📋 Instructions to Participants – Day of Presentation

1. Reporting

  • All teams must report at the venue at least 1 hour before their allotted presentation time.
  • Teams must remain available at the venue until their evaluation is completed.

2. Presentation Time

  • Each team will be given a maximum of 10 minutes for their presentation.
  • Teams must strictly adhere to the 10-minute time limit.
  • Judges may stop the presentation if the allotted time is exceeded.
  • Teams are advised to rehearse beforehand and ensure that their presentation is completed within the allotted time.

3. Presentation Setup

  • Carry the presentation in PPT/PDF format and keep a backup copy on a pen drive/cloud storage.
  • Ensure that the presentation can be accessed and displayed without technical difficulties.

4. GitHub Repository

  • The team's GitHub repository must be updated before the presentation.
  • Ensure that the README file is complete and provides all necessary information about the project.
  • The repository will be once again verified after the presentation.
  • Judges may refer to the GitHub repository as part of the evaluation process.

5. Team Participation

  • All team members should be familiar with the project and its implementation.
  • Judges may direct questions to any member of the team.
  • Team members should be able to clearly explain their individual contributions when asked.

6. Questions from Judges

  • Listen carefully to the questions and answer precisely and confidently.
  • If you do not know an answer, be honest rather than providing incorrect information.
  • Teams should be prepared for questions related to their technical approach, innovation, feasibility, scalability, security, cost, implementation, and future development.

7. Professional Conduct

  • Maintain professional decorum throughout the event.
  • Do not interrupt other teams during their presentations.
  • Follow the instructions of the coordinators, technical team, and judges.
  • Be respectful and professional while interacting with judges and other participants.
⚠️ Important

The presentation time is strictly limited to 10 minutes. Plan, rehearse, and manage your time accordingly.

Understand your problem. Present your solution clearly. Demonstrate your innovation. Be ready to answer questions and defend your technical decisions.
