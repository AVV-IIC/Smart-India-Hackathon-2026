Who's actually showing up: year of study

Joining year, read from each roll number's batch segment and its programme length (4-yr, 5-yr integrated, or 2-yr postgraduate), converted to year of study as of Aug 2026 (academic year 2026–27). Postgraduate participants are shown in their own row rather than folded into the undergraduate ladder.

2,737 of 2,748 resolved
75.1% of all participants are in their 1st or 2nd year — hackathon turnout skews heavily toward newer students.
4th-year students (2023 batch, final year on a 4-year B.Tech) make up just 2.0% of participants — 56 students across all 458 teams. 5th-year standing applies only to 2022-batch students on a 5-year integrated programme (Data Science, Maths & Computing, or BBA LL.B.) — no students from that batch appear in this dataset, so the row shows zero rather than being folded into "unresolved." Among students who are on one of those three 5-yr integrated programmes, the year-of-study spread is 1st year: 39, 2nd year: 44, 3rd year: 7, 4th year: 29 — confirming the batches present simply haven't reached year 5 yet, rather than year 5 being uncounted. A further 37 participants (1.3%) are postgraduate students (M.Tech / M.Sc., 2-year programmes) rather than undergraduates. The remaining 11 unresolved entries (0.4%) are roll numbers with a missing, truncated, or non-standard segment — genuinely unrecoverable from the data as submitted, not a rounding gap.
